## 1. Our Legal Duty & Obligations
Under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA), Minimally Invasive Spine & Brain Institute (MSBI) is required by law to:
- Maintain the privacy and security of your Protected Health Information (PHI).
- Provide you with this formal Notice of our legal duties and privacy practices regarding your health information.
- Notify you promptly following any breach of your unsecured health information.
- Abide by the terms of the Notice currently in effect.

---

## 2. How We May Use and Disclose Your Health Information

### A. For Treatment (T)
We may use and disclose your health information to physicians, nurses, technicians, and other clinical personnel involved in evaluating, diagnosing, and treating your spine or neurological condition.

### B. For Payment (P)
We may use and disclose your health information to bill and collect payment from health plans, insurance providers, or other third-party payers for the medical services you receive.

### C. For Health Care Operations (O)
We may use and disclose your health information for operational management, clinical quality assessment, staff training, licensing, and practice administration.

### D. Appointment Reminders & Inquiries
We may contact you via telephone, SMS, or email regarding appointment confirmations, consultation requests, or treatment alternatives, according to your indicated communication preferences.

---

## 3. Uses and Disclosures Requiring Your Written Authorization
- **Marketing Communications**: We will never sell your health information or disclose it to third parties for marketing purposes without your explicit, written HIPAA-compliant authorization.
- **Psychotherapy Notes & Highly Sensitive Records**: Any disclosure requires explicit authorization unless required by law.
- **Revocation**: You may revoke any written authorization at any time in writing.

---

## 4. Your Individual Rights Under the HIPAA Privacy Rule
1. **Right to Inspect and Copy**: You have the right to inspect and obtain an electronic or paper copy of your medical records.
2. **Right to Request Amendments**: If you believe health information we maintain is inaccurate or incomplete, you may request an amendment.
3. **Right to an Accounting of Disclosures**: You may request a list of disclosures made of your health information outside treatment, payment, or healthcare operations.
4. **Right to Request Restrictions**: You may request restrictions on how we use or disclose your information for treatment, payment, or operations. If you pay out-of-pocket in full, you may restrict disclosure to your health plan.
5. **Right to Confidential Communications**: You may request to receive communications by specific means (e.g. cell phone only) or at specific locations.
6. **Right to a Paper Copy of This Notice**: You may obtain a paper copy upon request at any time.
