# HIPAA MANUAL COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST
**System:** Spine Brain / Midwest Spine & Brain Institute (MSBI) Clinical Intelligence CRM  
**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Purpose:** Operational execution checklist for designated institutional owners to complete, sign, and maintain manual, legal, and organizational HIPAA compliance requirements.  

---

> [!NOTE]
> ### OPERATIONAL INSTRUCTIONS
> This checklist tracks administrative, physical, workforce, and contractual compliance controls. Upon execution of each task, the responsible owner must record the completion date, attach the designated evidence artifact to the corresponding folder in `compliance/evidence/`, and obtain the reviewer's signature.

---
