## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy defines the disciplinary framework and graduated sanction process applied to workforce members who fail to comply with security and privacy policies pursuant to **45 CFR § 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(C)**.

## 2. Violation Categories & Graduated Sanctions
1. **Level 1 — Minor / Unintentional (Negligent)**:
   - *Examples*: Leaving workstation unlocked, accidental misdirection of non-sensitive communication, minor failure to follow export naming conventions.
   - *Sanctions*: Documented verbal warning, mandatory security retraining, supervisor notification.
2. **Level 2 — Moderate / Repeat Negligence**:
   - *Examples*: Sharing passwords with colleagues, accessing ePHI beyond minimum necessary scope without clinical justification, repeated failure to lock sessions.
   - *Sanctions*: Written reprimand in HR personnel file, suspension of privileged system access, formal probationary period.
3. **Level 3 — Severe / Willful Misconduct**:
   - *Examples*: Intentional unauthorized disclosure of patient records, exfiltration of ePHI, selling or publicizing clinical data, installing malicious software, deliberate disabling of security controls.
   - *Sanctions*: Immediate termination of employment/contract, revocation of all access, referral for civil/criminal prosecution under HIPAA statutory enforcement.

## 3. Due Process & Reporting
- Technical audit logs (`ActivityLog`) provide forensic evidence.
- Final sanction decisions require formal HR, Privacy Officer, and Legal review.
