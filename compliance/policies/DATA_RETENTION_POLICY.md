## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy defines retention periods and secure disposal requirements for medical inquiries, call recordings, audit logs, compliance evidence, and operational data.

## 2. Retention Schedules
| Data Category | Minimum Retention Period | Legal/Regulatory Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HIPAA Compliance Policies & Evidence** | **6 Years** from creation / last effective date | **45 CFR § 164.316(b)(2)(i)** |
| **Security Audit Logs (`ActivityLog`)** | **6 Years** | HIPAA Security Rule Standard |
| **Patient Inquiries & Leads** | Defined by state medical board & clinical rules | `LEGAL/COMPLIANCE REVIEW REQUIRED` |
| **Call Recordings (`CallLog` audio URLs)** | Defined by clinical/operational policy | `LEGAL/COMPLIANCE REVIEW REQUIRED` |
| **User Session Records (`UserSession`)** | 90 Days post-revocation/expiry | Operational security cleanup |
| **Financial & Invoicing Records** | 7 Years | IRS / Financial Accounting Standards |

## 3. Secure Destruction Standards
- Electronic records past retention: Cryptographic erasure or NIST SP 800-88 media sanitization.
- Physical documents: Secure cross-cut shredding with certificate of destruction.
