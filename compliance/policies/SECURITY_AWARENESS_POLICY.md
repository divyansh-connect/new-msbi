## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy mandates regular information security awareness and privacy training for all workforce members with access to the Spine-Brain application pursuant to **45 CFR § 164.308(a)(5)**.

## 2. Policy Mandates
1. **Mandatory Onboarding Training**: All new employees and contractors must complete HIPAA Security and Privacy training within 14 days of hire before being granted access to production ePHI.
2. **Annual Refresher Course**: All active workforce members must complete annual refresher training.
3. **Core Training Curriculum**:
   - ePHI Handling & Minimum Necessary access rules
   - Strong Password practices & credential non-sharing
   - Phishing, Social Engineering & Smishing defense
   - Clean Desk & Workstation Security rules
   - Incident Reporting protocols & reporting suspicious activities
   - Data Export & removable storage prohibitions
   - Unauthorized disclosure penalties & workforce sanctions
4. **Documentation & Tracking**: All training completions, dates, trainer identities, and employee acknowledgments must be recorded in `SECURITY_TRAINING_RECORD.md`.
