## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy establishes the overarching information security framework for the **Spine-Brain Intelligence System** operated for the Minimally Invasive Spine & Brain Institute (MSBI). It governs the protection, confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all Electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) in compliance with the **HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR Part 160 and Part 164, Subparts A and C)**.

## 2. Policy Statements
1. **Administrative Safeguards**: The organization shall establish formal security management processes, assign defined security responsibilities, enforce workforce clearance/termination protocols, conduct regular risk analyses, and maintain incident response runbooks.
2. **Physical Safeguards**: Physical and cloud infrastructure hosting ePHI must maintain restricted physical access controls, environmental protections, and secure workstation usage rules.
3. **Technical Safeguards**: All systems processing ePHI must enforce unique user identification, role-based access control (RBAC), end-to-end TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, automatic session termination, and immutable audit logging.
4. **Non-Certification Acknowledgment**: Technical controls provide software enforcement capabilities; organizational compliance requires ongoing human governance, legal review, and workforce training.

## 3. Enforcement & Exceptions
- Violations of this policy are subject to workforce sanctions under `POL-SANCTIONS-013`.
- Any temporary exceptions require written approval from the Security Officer and Incident Commander.
