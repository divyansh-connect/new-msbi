**Governance Scope:** 45 CFR § 164.502(e) & § 164.504(e) (Business Associate Contracts)  

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### BAA VERIFICATION & GOVERNANCE POLICY
> In accordance with HIPAA verification standards, Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) must be evidenced by executed, countersigned legal contracts. Vendors processing or storing electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) require a signed BAA prior to production data transmission. Vendors processing strictly de-identified, aggregate, or administrative non-PHI data do not require a BAA provided technical guardrails prevent PHI transmission.

## Summary of BAA Execution Requirements

1. **CRITICAL PRODUCTION BLOCKER**: Execute Business Associate Agreement with **Railway Corp.** (or cloud infrastructure host) prior to storing live production ePHI in MySQL.
2. **HIGH PRIORITY**: Execute Healthcare Business Associate Agreement with **CallRail, Inc.** prior to ingesting patient phone calls or audio recordings into CRM.
3. **POLICY ENFORCEMENT**: Maintain strict code guardrails ensuring Twilio SMS, SendGrid Email, Google Ads, GA4, Meta Ads, and Mailchimp receive zero patient demographic or health data.
