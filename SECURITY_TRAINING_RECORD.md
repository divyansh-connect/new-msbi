## 2. Training Verification & Signoff Procedure
1. Upon completing the certified HIPAA awareness module, the workforce member signs a written/electronic acknowledgment.
2. The Compliance Trainer records the certificate ID and completion date in this register.
3. Annual automated reminder alerts are dispatched 30 days prior to the renewal due date.
