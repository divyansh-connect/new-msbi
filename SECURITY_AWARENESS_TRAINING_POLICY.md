## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy mandates formal security awareness and privacy training for all workforce personnel with access to the Spine-Brain platform pursuant to **45 CFR § 164.308(a)(5)**.

## 2. Mandatory Training Curriculum
1. **HIPAA Security & Privacy Fundamentals**: Defining ePHI, Covered Entity vs Business Associate relationships, and minimum necessary access.
2. **Password & Credential Security**: Constructing strong passphrases (> 12 chars), prohibition against password sharing, and recognizing session hijacking.
3. **Phishing & Social Engineering Defense**: Identifying spear-phishing, spoofed email domains, suspicious webhook alerts, and malicious links.
4. **Clean Desk & Workstation Security**: Auto-locking screens when stepping away, physical security in clinical environments, and preventing shoulder surfing.
5. **Secure Remote Access**: Restrictions against public Wi-Fi without VPN, zero usage of unencrypted personal devices for ePHI.
6. **Security Incident Reporting**: Immediate reporting procedures for suspected malware, accidental disclosures, or lost mobile devices (`POL-INC-008`).
7. **Data Export & Media Controls**: Restrictions against exporting patient inquiries to personal flash drives or cloud storage.
8. **Workforce Sanctions & Disciplinary Consequences**: Explanation of the graduated sanction framework (`POL-SNC-013`).

## 3. Training Administration & Documentation
- Training records must be maintained for **6 years** in `SECURITY_TRAINING_RECORD.md`.
- No employee is granted access to live patient records until initial training completion is verified by the Compliance Officer.
